About Workflow

Simplify Your Work. Organize Your Projects. Achieve Your Goals.

Smart Workflow is a web-based project and task management application designed to help teams organize projects, assign responsibilities, track task progress, and collaborate efficiently in one place.

The application provides role-based access for Project Managers, Team Leaders, and Team Members, ensuring that everyone can manage and monitor their work according to their responsibilities.

How Smart Workflow Works

1

Create Your Account

Register with your details, select your role, and log in to access your personalized dashboard.

2

Manage Projects

Project Managers can create projects, provide project descriptions, and organize team members around project goals.

3

Build Your Team

Authorized Project Managers and Team Leaders can add eligible colleagues from the same company to a project.

4

Create and Assign Tasks

Project Managers and Team Leaders can create tasks, set priorities, select assignees, and define task statuses to keep work organized.

5

Track Task Progress

Monitor tasks based on their current status. View ongoing tasks by default and switch to completed tasks whenever you need to review finished work.

6

Collaborate with Your Team

View project members, check assigned responsibilities, and keep project activities organized in one centralized workspace.

7

Manage Your Profile

Update your personal details, contact information, date of birth, and profile picture to keep your account information up to date.

User Roles and Responsibilities

Project Manager

The Project Manager is responsible for creating projects and overseeing their organization and progress.

  • Create and manage projects.
  • Add team members to projects.
  • Create and assign tasks.
  • Set task priorities and monitor task progress.
  • Coordinate project activities and ensure work stays organized.

Team Leader

The Team Leader helps coordinate project activities and supports team members in completing assigned work.

  • Participate in assigned projects.
  • Add eligible members to projects where authorized.
  • Create and assign tasks.
  • Help coordinate team activities.
  • Monitor assigned tasks and support project progress.

Team Member

The Team Member role includes all employees who are not assigned the Project Manager or Team Leader role, regardless of their job title or experience level.

Examples of team members include:

  • Senior Developers
  • Junior Developers
  • Software Developers
  • Frontend and Backend Developers
  • Testers and QA Engineers
  • Interns and Trainees
  • Other team employees who are not Project Managers or Team Leaders

Responsibilities:

  • View projects and tasks available to them.
  • Access tasks assigned to them.
  • Update task information where permitted.
  • Track ongoing and completed tasks.
  • Collaborate with colleagues to achieve project goals.

In Smart Workflow, job titles and experience levels do not determine the application role. For example, both Senior Developers and Junior Developers are considered Team Members unless they are assigned the Project Manager or Team Leader role.

Why Choose Smart Workflow?

  • Centralized Management: Keep projects, tasks, and team information in one place.
  • Clear Responsibilities: Assign tasks to appropriate team members and establish clear ownership.
  • Progress Tracking: Easily distinguish ongoing tasks from completed tasks.
  • Role-Based Access: Provide access according to each user's responsibilities.
  • Team Collaboration: Help team members coordinate their work and stay informed about project activities.
  • Simple Interface: Navigate between projects, team members, tasks, and profile settings with ease.

Our Goal

Our goal is to make project management simpler, improve team coordination, and help users complete their work efficiently through a clear and organized workflow.
